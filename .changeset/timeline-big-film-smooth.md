Zooming and scrolling the timeline of a big film stay smooth. Only the tracks near the view have their clips drawn, so a film with hundreds of sounds on dozens of tracks lays out a fraction of them. A pinch or Ctrl+wheel zoom scales what is drawn while you zoom and lays the timeline out once you stop, keeping the moment under the pointer where it is. Scrolling up and down moves the track heads with the tracks without redrawing the timeline.
