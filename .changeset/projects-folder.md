Projects now shows every project in your projects folder, one folder each, including the ones an agent made there or you copied in; one you delete from the folder leaves the list. Projects you opened from elsewhere stay in the list too, and a project's … menu has Reveal in Finder (Explorer on Windows) to show where its folder is. Settings → General → Storage calls it the projects folder. When you change it, or set it back to the default, Studio asks whether your projects move with it: moved, each folder goes whole (across disks it is copied, then the old one goes to the Trash); left behind, they stay where they are and in the list.
