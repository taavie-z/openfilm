CodeBuddy works as its China edition: it is started that way unless your shell sets CODEBUDDY_INTERNET_ENVIRONMENT yourself, and its install link is the China site's. A turn an agent gives up on now shows the agent's own reason (CodeBuddy's network error, say) instead of an empty reply. Signing in in Terminal runs in a folder of OpenFilm's own, so CodeBuddy no longer warns about trusting your home folder. Two models an agent names alike (CodeBuddy's "Hy3") are told apart in the menu.
