A turn whose agent waits five minutes on its model with nothing coming (CodeBuddy, its connection hung) is stopped and says why. Why a turn failed still shows after OpenFilm restarts: the last one's above the composer, an older one's on its Interrupted. Agents are asked whether they are signed in less often, in a folder of OpenFilm's own instead of your home folder, and none is left running after OpenFilm quits.
