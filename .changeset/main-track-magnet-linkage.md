Studio's timeline: the magnet keeps the main track (the lowest picture track) closed up. A clip put on it goes in between, a deleted one closes the gap, and a trim or a new speed moves what follows. Linkage keeps the titles, overlays and sounds over a main-track clip on their moment of it, and deletes them with it; a clip that starts past the end of the main track rides on nothing. Dragging the cut between two clips trims the clip you grab. The new preview axis shows the frame under the pointer as it moves over the timeline; its switch sits in the toolbar beside the magnet, snapping and linkage. Keys: P (or ⇧N) magnet, ~ linkage, S preview axis.
